## Page Text Content

```text
ANNUAL
SWIMMING,GALA （78-79）
LHE Swimming Gala, 1978/1979 was held on 15th September 1978 in the Morrison Hill
Swimming Pool.The final day was neld on 20th September.
This year more students took part in the competition and the result, on the whole，
was unexpectedly good. Three new records （all in A Grade） were made and they were as
follows: 50 metres Back Stroke, 100 metres Breast Stroke, 200 metres Breast Stroke.
```
