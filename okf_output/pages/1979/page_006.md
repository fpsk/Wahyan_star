## Page Text Content

```text
DMIHOAET
PRINCIPAL'S
MESSAGE
THE year 1979 reminds us that Wah Yan College is now 60 years old，
having been opened on December 10th.， 1919. Our founder, Mr. Peter
Tsui Yan Sau is, happily, still with us, having celebrated his ninetieth birthday
some years ago. The oldest of our past students are beginning to retire from
active life and some ofYthem have grandsons now in the school. It is such
ties as these that help so much to build up the sense of family life in the
school, communicating itself to all who join Wah Yan and make them feel，
on leaving, that they are leaving home.
The past year is worthy of being the Diamond Jubilee Year. The breadth
and quality of educational activities have culminated in some outstanding
performances in widely different fields. The Certificate Examination results
of the year 1978 were the best ever. Our basketball and badminton teams
were overall champions in Division 1 of Hong Kong Island and the school
won the Jean Cassels' Trophy for the school with the best record in donating
blood during the year.
In addition to greater contact by overseas past students with their mother-
school, it is pleasing to report much activity and much cooperation with the
school on the part of the Past Students Association.
Wah Yan has much to be thankful to God for during its 60 years history，
but mention must be made, in particular, of the loyalty of its past, its teachers
and the many members of its staff that have served so long in the school.
Principal.
```
