## Page Text Content

```text
BLOOD DONATION
OUR school,has a very good tradition in blood
donating. In tie past few years, we have constantly
been among the most gqenerous students in giving
blood. his year, the result was partcularly encour
aging. The frst blood donation was held on 9th
September, 1978 in the School Hall. The second blood
donation was also in the School Hall on 23rd March.
Altogether, 86% of qualifed donors gave their blood.
This was the highest percentage in all the schools in
H.K. For this, we were awarded the Mrs. Cassel's
Award. Giving blood is a most valuable thing. Blood
is something which you have more than enough but
which for others is a matter of life of death. We hope
this tradition will be taken up by the younger boys.
```
