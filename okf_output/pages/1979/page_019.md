SCHOOL CHOIR
For tne Choir, this year passed quietly.
only. We had given performance
only once within the school on speech
dlay. With the motive of arousing
our students' interest in classical music.
the music library established last Year
was extended by some keen members of
the Choir, lunch time concerts were
held; cassettes were lent to students.
In March, we entered the Inter-
mediate Boys' Choir Competition in
the 3Ist School Music Festival.With a
terribly exhausting piece ‘Santa Lucia'，
we got 82 marks （a satisfactory though
not wonderful result）.
Finally, we would like to express
our sincere gratitude to Mr. Lam for
his direction in this year.
SCHOOL ORCHESTRA
Although the orchestra consists only
of 7 members, it is quite active. This
was a successtul year for the orchestra
and we have tried hard to promote
classical music among the students. As
a result, many students began playing
musical instruments such as the violin
or the fute. Besides having routine
practice, the orchestra gave three per-
formances this year. Two were in our
school, on Speech Day and at the
Christmas Celebration, and one was in
Wah Yan College Kowloon during its
Cultural Night.
