Trama Festival :78-79.
This year, the Intermediate and the Senior Sections of the Drama Festival were held
between November and December. Fr. Reid and Fr. McCarthy were the adjudicators for
the Intermediate and Senior Sections respectively. In the beginning of May, the Junior
Section was organised with Fr. O'Rourke as the judge. The standard was quite high.
RESULTS
Senior Section：
Best Play: One Night in November （5Al）
Best Actor: Weh Yeh Shing as Toffy in A
Night at an Inn. （5B）
C
Intermediate Section：
Best Play: The Troublesome Man （4B1）
Best Actor: Chan Kwok Lai as Scuttleboom
in Scuttleboom's Treasure （4Al）
Junior Section：
Best Play: The Second Wish （2B2）
directed by Ephraem Tsui and
Shek Ming Tung （L6A）
Best Actor: Glen Griffths as the King in The
Second Wish （2B2）
The aim of the Drama Festival is to promote the students' interest in the art of drama
and class spirit through the cooperation among students during the preparation work. We
firmly believe that our aims were fully achieved. The Drama Festival was a success.
