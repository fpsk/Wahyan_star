DC-DMTA8SC
DRAMA SOCIETY
Adviser: Fr. R. MicCarthy, S.J.
Chairman: Patrick Chan
OUR club did not have many activities
this year. This was due to the fact
that we had a very small membership.
The Intermediate and the Senior
Sections of the Drama Festival were held
in the middle and at the end of Novem-
ber respectively. The standard of these
tWo sections was quite high. Just before
the drama competitions, the exco-members
of our club helped the school to renew
the “Alats” used on the stage and repaired
some of the properties backstage.
The Junior Drama Festival was held
at the end of April. Students from L.6
and F.4 were invited to be the directors
of the plays of F.l and 2. It was a
SuCCesS.
At the end of March, the Chinese
Society of St. Paul Convent's School and
our club jointly put on a play at the
concert - “Kaleidoscope 331". It was a
humorous drama and received great praise.
The Councillors’ Club of Belilios
Public School and our club jointly parti-
cipated in the Joint School Broadcasting
Contest organised by Wah Yan College，
Kowloon in April. A broadcasting drama
was produced by the two clubs for the
contest. We did not win the competition
but we gained much experience.
We would like to thank our adviser，
Fr. McCartny, S.J. for his invaluable
advice throughout the year and express
our gratitude to all the students who
helped in the activities of our club in this
academic year.
