```text
Adviser: Rev. Fr. D. Reid, S.J.
Chairman: Charles Lee
BRIDGE CLUB
THE executive committee was formed in
October and our frst function was a bridge
training course for beginners. The course
lasted for one month. During the course, the
members were taught the basic techniques of
playing bridge, including both the theory and
practices. We had our practices every Tuesday
and Friday.
On 4th December, a friendly match was
held in our School. Three schools were invited，
namely, Sacred Heart Canossian College, St.
Paul's Secondary School and Wah Yan College
Kowloon. Over ten teams took part in this
match and we all enjoyed an excellent morn-
ing. In the afternoon, on the same day, the
Annual Open Pairs Tournament was held.
About seventy took part and refreshments were
provided
The Annual Internal Pairs Tournament
was held on 30th Dec.， started at 9:30 a.m.and
over thirty took part in this function. Most
ended at 1:00 p.m. It was free of charge and
of the participants were from the upper forms
but their results were just fair. The scores
were calculated soon after the match.
Results: Ist Anthony Yuen, Dominic Pang
2nd Charles Lee, Peter Lo
3rd Alex Lam, Vincent Yu
Competing in the Tactics Bowl organized
by the J.S.B.A.，two of our teams got amongst
the top twelve （totally 60 teams）. They entered
the fnal and one of them came second and was
awarded a book coupon of $150.
This year, some books on Bridge ethics
and systems were bought. We hope that these
books will help our members in playing Bridge.
Lastly, we would like to express our sincere
ggratitude to our Adviser, Rev. Fr. Reid, S.J.
for his guidance throughout the year.
```
