## Page Text Content

```text
STUDENT FESTIVAL '79
SiENO
ICES
AAN COLLEGE
our Student Festival. The theme of it
This year was the seventh time we held
was providing a fortnight of various programmes for the students and simultaneously
stressing the Importance of politeness among the students. The duration was two weeks，
trom 17th March to 3lst March.
The programmes of the Festival were jointly organised by the Student Council，
Social Service Society, Chinese Society, Music Society, Cine Club, and Tennis Club. The
beginning of the Festival was Walk 317, which was followed by the Monday Theatre （Butch
Cassidy and the Sun Dance Kit）， an interhouse Competition named Tuesday Delight, Mixed
Debate with St. Paul's Secondary School and our school, a Book Exhibition, an Orienteering
named Operation 324, a Joint School Social Exhibition, an Invitation tennis Tournament
between students and teachers, the Thursday Theatre （Golden Age Comedy）. Finally came
the climax of the Festival, the Teachers' Day and the concert, Kaleidescope 331. Moreover，
VISitS, lunch time concerts, and film shows were held
Lastly, we wish to express our warmest thanks to those who have helped to make
the Student Festival '79 a success.
```
