## Page Text Content

```text
THE ISTh HONG KONG
SCOUTS_
Group History
1926- Scouting began in Wah Yan. The Scout Troop was registered as the 28th Hong
Kong （Wah Yan） Troop, and was re-registered as the 15th Hong Kong （Wah Yan）
Troop later in the year.
1932- The Rover Crew was formed.
1937 - Our scouts took part in the closing ceremonies and procession of the Catholic Action
Congress held in Canton.
1941 - Scout activities were suspended in December due to World War II.
1946- Scouting resumed in Wah Yan. The I5th Hong Kong Group （Wah Yan College）
was formed on I6th April.
1949 - The Senior Scout Troop was formed in June.
- Raymond Yue, G.S.M，， was awarded the Cross of Gallentry.
1951- One of our scouts was selected as a members of the Hong Kong Contingent to the
World Scout Jamboree in Austria.
- Ho Shiu Hay and Chan Kwok Chak （senior scouts） were awarded the Cross of
Gallentry, presented by the Governor, for their bravery in saving a child in a squatter
fire.
1952- Sea Scouting began in Wah Yan with the formation of the Senior Sea Scout Troop
on 5th September.
- Five members of the Group attended the Pan-Pacific Jamboree in Australia.
1953- The Cub Pack was formed in March.
1955 - The College moved from Robinson Road to• Mt. Parish.
- The Group Committee was formed.
1957- One member of the Group attended the Jubilee Jamboree in England.
-A combined camp, known as the “Wah Yan Scouts Camp”， with the IIth Kowloon
Group of Wah Yan College, Kowloon was held in Stanley.
The Group presented the Ist Wah Yan Scout Week with the Ist Wah Yan Scout Show.
1958- The past scouts of the Group formed the Old Scout Troop.
- The Group presented the 2nd Wai Yan Scout Show.
1959- Members of the Group participated in the 1Oth World Jamboree in the Philippines，
1961- The Cub Pack was closed because the College discontinued the primary classes.
- The Group participated in the Jubilee Jamborette in Kowloon Tsai Park in cele-
bration of the 50th Anniversary of Scouting in Hong Kong.
```
