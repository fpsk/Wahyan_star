SPEECH
YAOH
DAY
｜ever would the graduates forget that great day, 26th
N November, 1976, the day our Annual Speedi Day was held.
This year we were glad to have Mr. Lee Chun Yon, the
head of the Social Welfare Department, to be our guest and
Mrs. Lee to give us the certificates. The ceremony began at
5:30 p.m.
After our Assistant Principal, Mr. Yu, had welcomed our
guests, our Principal, Fr. Barrett, delivered his speech. He
told us how the early students of Wah Yan had shown their
regard for the poor. During the War in 1939, refugees kept
pouring in Hong Kong. They needed help, and our Doys
answered them.
It is encouraging now to see Wah Yan
students still making efforts in social work. Lastly, Fr. Barrett
reminded us that we should not be working for credit from
others, but from care for others.
Next came the presentation of certificates and prizes.
Sure every graduate had an unforgettable moment when he
received the certificate from Mrs. Lee's hands.
Then Mr. Lee, who had studied and also taught in Wah
Yan, delivered to us his address. His subject was social work.
He said that social work, as a career, was comparatively new
in Hong Kong. Due to rapid economic growth, many social
problems such as the generation gap, unemployment and vice
appeared. They were waiting to he solved by social workers.
To be a social worker, one should equip oneself with adequate
knowledge, and should have love for others. Mr. Lee also
addea that a social worker should work from interest but not
for riches.
The ceremony ended with a vote of thanks given by the
Head Prefect. And we had a performance given by our school
orchestra and choir.
