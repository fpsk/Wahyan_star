GROUP GOLDEN JUBILEE 1926-1976
1962 - The Old Scout Troop was officially registered as the 15th Hong Kong Group （Wah
Yan College） Branch of the B.P. Scout Guild of London.
1963- Ihe Sea Scout Section of the Group was closed, due to lack of scouters.
1965- The Group presented the 2nd Wah Yan Scout Week.
1966- Two members of the Group attended the 4th Nippon National Jamboree in Japan.
1967- One member of the Group participated in the I2th World Scout Jamboree in U.S.A.
1969 - Our venture scouts appeared on TVB and gave a demonstration of scouting skill.
1970- One venture scout was selected as a member of Hong Kong's team to the Asia-
Pacific Scout Youth Conference in Japan. He also took part in the Expo Scout
Day and the 5th Nippon National Jamboree.
1971- The Group participated in the Jamboree in Castle Peak in celebration of the
Diamond Jubilee of Scouting in Hong Kong, and issued special First Day Covers
in connection with the issue of stamps in commemoration of the occasion.
- The Pun U Association Wah Yan Primary School was opened in September. The
Group started a Cup Scout Pack there，
- Our Group Scarf was altered with the addition of the Scout Badge on the tip of
the Scarf.
- One member of the Group took part the 13th World Scout Jamboree in Japan.
1972- Our scouts appeared on RTV. We demonstrated the Investiture Ceremony, per.
formed a short play and sang two songs.
1973- The Scout Group and the Venture Scout Unit spent two days in the Police Cadet
School in Sheung Shui for Foot Drill training conducted by Officers of the said School.
1974- One venture scout was selected as a member of Hong Kong's team to the 6th Nippon
National Scheme in Japan.
1976- The Group presented the Brd Wah Yan Scout Show in celebration of the Golden
Jubilee of Scouting in Wah Yan.
