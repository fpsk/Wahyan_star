Functions held in this academic year：
1. Friendly match between teachers and
students.
2. Interclass Chinese Chess Competition.
3. Chinese Chess Competition.
We joined the Joint School Chess Association, and our chairman was elected the
chairman of the J.S.C.A. A few interschool chess competitions were organised by the
Association, but we had only taken part in one of them. This was because our members
were a bit below standard. We hope that the members will show more interest in the
interschool competitions in the coming year.

This year, the Club was quite a success.
Altogether 2 competitions and an exhibition were organized. The frst competition
was the Christmas Cards Design Competition held in November. The second was the
Art Competition in May. The response in both was very good and the winners were
awarded with book tokens. An exhibition was also held on 13th & l4th May. More
than 40 articles were displayed, which included drawings, paintings, designs and some
relief as well.
A social gathering was held on 28th May for the members of the Chinese Club，
the History Club and ours. More than 50 students enjoyed a nice afternoon.
Finally, we would like to express our thanks to our adviser, Mrs. Ho, for her valu-
able advice throughout the year.
