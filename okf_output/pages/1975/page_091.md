Bowling Team
Our team consisted of 4 members. We were among the eight that represented
South China bowling team to meet the Philippine Youth team that visited Hong Kong
in April. Ours was a strong team and thus got smoothly into the final. We thought we
could win the championship if one of the players had been careful enough. We had
to be satisfied with the Ist runners-up with only 4 points less! In the individual com-
petition, Edmund Ting got the 2nd runners-up.
It was a gloomy year for the ping pong Players. The
‘A'and C' grade players didn't do well and couldn't
enter the fnal. The B' grade team struggled hard and
got the 2nd runners-up in the league competition.
