## Page Text Content

```text
Our Bishop, Msgr. Lei died on the 23, July, 1974. We had lost not only a
Bishop, but a dear friend to all Christian, still more, a true friend of us - the young
Christians.
Bishop Lei was of the old generation, brought up and educated in the traditions
of the Catholic Church, but for him there was no "generation gap" no lack of under-
standing and appreciation of the needs and aspirations of the younger generation. He
therefore, devoted much of his time to young people, paying regular personal visits
to the homes of people to advise and help them in whatever way was necessary. In-
deed, he wanted to make the Church in Hongkong the kind of Church to which youths
would like to belong.
Though he has left us, he still remains present through his example. We had
a model to emulate. His prayer life, his spirit of sacrifice and service, his spirit of
faith, his simplicity of life, his spirit of poverty, his fidelity to God's call, his unquali-
fied loyalty to the Church, this is his spiritual legacy to us. We hoped that through
profting by his example, we might choose the way - the correct one - to follow
Christ.
As the preface of the Mass confidently states， "life is changed, not ended."
Bishop Lei has entered a new and fuller life, which is as real as the one he had left，
but endless and without sorrow. As he shared in Christ's sufferings, he now shares in
his consolations; as he shared in Christ's labour, he now shares in his reward; as he
shared in Christ's death, he now shares in his life. Christ promised that， “He who
believes in me, even if he dies, will live for ever.” So let us pray to our Lord that our
Bishop will share His everlasting happiness.
```
