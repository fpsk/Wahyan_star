```text
Basketball Annual Report
It was a disastrous year for Wah Yan in interschool sports. It could not be an
exception in the field of basketball. We could not even get a single medal in the A，
B, and G grade teams.
Although we remained undefeated in
the frst round, we lost three matches in
the finals, namely to Lingnam Middle
School, International School and New
Method College. The excuse of the failure
is our lack of experience and inferiority
in height and physical strength - our
Opponents were often a few inches taller
than us ! Thus our position was the fourth
- a result which was far from satisfac-
tion.
A GRADE
We failed to retain the championship
of last year no matter how hard did we
try. We had the same destiny as our A
grade team - the fourth.
B GRADE
In fact, we were one of the favourites
at the beginning, but we do not know
how we could have lost two decisive
matches in the first round, so we could
not even enter the final.
We would like to thank Mr. Lee Mui
Sang and Mr. Wong Pak Ho, our
coaches, who taught us not only skill, but
gallantry, fghting spirit as well as sports-
manship.
C GRADE
```
