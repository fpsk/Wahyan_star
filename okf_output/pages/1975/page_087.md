REPORT OF BADMINTON
This year we have a strong team -
all the members were experienced and
skilful shuttlers. With the aid of our
coach Mr. Wong Fai Hung, we practis
ed hard. We got through easily in the
first round, beating the others with in-
spiring results. We thought that we
could win the championship but fate was
against us. In the final round, we lost a
decisive game to St. Paul Boys and secur~
ed only the runners-up.
A GRADE
We didn't do well this year. Our
◎
team members were green both in skill
and experience. Though we struggled
hard, we couldn't enter the final round.
I hope that we can do better next year.
B GRADE
Our team consisted of boys from F.l
and F.2. We lacked both experience as
well as physical strength. Though with
the help of Brother Chan and the A
grade players, we couldn't enter the final.
Our opponents were too strong for us！
We would like to thank Rev. Fr.
Corbally for his guidience; Mr. Wong
Fai Hung, our coach, despite his work
business work came to teach us every
Saturday, and Mr. Lee, Mr. Cheung for
being our teachers-in-charge.
C GRADE
