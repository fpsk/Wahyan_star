FROM THE PRINCIPAL
Fr. C. J. Barrett, S.J.
Our new Rector, Fr. Fergus Croni, S.J. O.B.E，， J.P. was appointed on August Ist
1974 to take the place of Fr. M. Corbally, S.J. who fortunately remamned with us as
Minister and also Sportsmaster. Fr. Cronin is an old member of the Wah Yan teach-
ing staff having been with us in Robinson Road in 1936-37 and later in Queen's Road
East from 1955 till 58. Unfortunately, when he came to us in August "74 he was ill
and was soon under treatment in the Queen Mary Hospital. Just before Christmas
he was sent to Hammersmith Hospital, London where he has continued to make the
progress which he began in Queen Mary Hospital. We confidently hope to have him
back with us completely cured before September "75.
On September 2nd, Pun U Wah Yan Primary School opened and on the 6th，
Wah Yan College began classes with 160 new boys in Form I including 95 from
But for-
P.U.W.Y. Primary School. Classes began with the threat of water rationing.
tunately, before the end of October some friendly' typhoons had come close enough
to begin filling our reservoirs and thus banishing the fear of water shortage. Best of
all, they had not come too close to cause notable damage.
These were staff changes: Mr. Robert Chung retired after a lifetime of devoted
service; Sister Mary B. Duane left to prepare to start her own new school; Mr. P. H.
Wong, a P.E. graduate from Taiwan is taking Mr. Chung's place. Beginning this year
Form 3 and 4 are to have double Physical Education lessons to allow more time for
training and games.
On November 11th, Confrmation was given in the School Chapel to 30 Primary
and 21 Secondary students by the Vicar Capitular of the Diocese then, Msgr. Gabriel
Lam Cheuk Wai. On November 22nd, Msgr. Lam gave the address on our Speech
Day and distributed the certificates.
This year our Blood Donations numbered 152 in February.
March the 16th was Open Day. It was organised by the Student Council and
was a real success. It was all brought about by the unaided eftorts of our students.
We thank them very heartily.
The number of Kong Tak Yan Bursaries awarded during the year increased to
80.
Of our 1974 Matriculation classes, 20 are in Hong Kong University, 6 in Medi-
cine,3 in Science, 1 in Engineering, 4 in Social Science, 6 in Arts, 5 are in the Hong
Kong Polytechnic. There are 13 in Universities abroad.
