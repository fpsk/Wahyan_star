Ping Pong Tournament
The second pingpong tournament was hosted by our mother school this year.
There were Wah Yan H.K.， Wah Yan, Kowloon, Poon U
Wah Yan Primary School, Ricci Hall Wah Yanites, Kowloon Wah Yan Past Students
Association Ltd. and our own association. The convenor of the tournament was still
Mr. Cheng Fook Choy, our sports secretary. The champion this year is Kowloon Wah
Yan Students and the H.K. Students as runnersup while Ricci Hall Wah Yanites
cupies the 3rd chair. A prospective tournament will be held this November at Kowloon
Wah Yan College. Get into action now.
Congratulation Dinner
A dinner party was held in Wing Lung Bank Dining Hall in September in
congratulating Mr. Augustine Chui Kam for his promotion as Deputy Director in the
Department of Home Affairs.
He is a past student of Wah Yan. His ability and success are what we are to aim
for.
Graduation Dinner
The Annual Speech Day falls in November and the dinner was arranged，
usual, in the hall of Wah Yan where the graduates enjoyed delicious meals with their
teachers and many past students. The high light was not the Shark's fin but was a
game of riddles organised by our Liaison Officer Mr. John Lam. He, himself, also
donated 2 cartons of milk as prizes for the winners.

Spring Party
Following last years’success, we held our spring dinner party at Franho Restaur-
ant again. Cheers and greetings filled up the hall that night in February.
Swimming Shed
We have created a roof terrace to our shed this year and a memorable opening
ceremony was held in May.
The participants enjoyed a good afternoon in a sunny
beach and warm sea water with refreshment provided free by our President.
Career Talks
The Association had intended to discuss the various interesting careers with the
students again this year. But we regret that we have to postpone the talks until later
in the year with the consent of the College to allow time for the examinees to pave
their way for the future.
The Association is in the progress of arranging for its next annual general
meeting and it is hoped that the new office bearers so elected will work up more ener-
ggetic activities for the Association.
