ANNUAL BALL '74
This year, the Annual Ball was held on the 7th of December, 1974. It started
at 7:30 p.m. Ladies from St. Stephen's Girls' School and St. Francis Canossian College
were invited. More than one hundred students from F.4 to F.6 of our school particip-
ated. There were altogether two bands and refreshments from Windsor Restaurant were
served.
As a whole, the Ball was quite successful and it was an enjoyable evening. We
would like to thank Mr. Raymond Yu, our assistant principal who has given us his valu-
able advice.
