Sports Revieu of Year 74-75
This year various interclass and interhouse games were organized as usual and
they were well carried out. The students were very enthusiastic. Many participated，
but very few came to cheer.
In the field of interschool sports, the results were not satisfactory. There was
not enough New Blood' to replace those who left. Fr. Corbally, when he resumed the
post of sportsmaster in '72, said that he would get the school team players properly
trained. He did so by inviting coaches for them. However our results in interschool
sports were still down.
What do we lack? Responsibility, I should say. Most interschool players were
too self-centered. They didn't consider it an honour of being school representatives.
This could be seen from the attendence records. Their common excuses were- I had
a lot of work to do; I had an assignment; I had to attend an important meeting....
etc. It happened once that one school team had to give up a match because one player
did not turn up！
Despite the excellent coaches that we have, deteat was inevitable if we show no
responsibility. Here I would like to thank those who have offered their help and
support during this year.
