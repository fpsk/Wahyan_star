VENTURE SCOUTS
The programming and administration
of the unit were fully entrusted
Executive Committee which consisted of
five elected members and the leaders of the
unit who were official members. Three
venture scouts, Chan Chi Keung, Chow
Keung Kun and Ng Fook Hoi attended a
Leadership Training Course run by the
Regional Headquarters. The application
for the Venture Scout Award for Lum Yuk
Sun was approved and he was the first ven-
ture scout of the unit to get that award.
SCOUTS
The troop had the assistance of two
new leaders, Yung Wing Sang and Ng Pak
Leung. A troop week-end camp was held
on 10.4.71 to 11.4.71, at Cheung Sha Near
the YWCA Youth Camp. A patrol of six
scouts had enrolled for the Eastern District
Competition to be held on 22-23rd May.
If they win, they will be qualified for the
Regional Competition and then the Colony-
wide Carlton Trophy Competition.
Scouts Camp Gate
Scout Troop
