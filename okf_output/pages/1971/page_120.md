This year, our annual Swimming Gala was successfully held on 30th September, 1970

events were held within four hours in the morning. Two records were broken, C grade 50
metres free style and C grade 100 metres free style. Both were set by Cheung Kam Kwong.
The A, B, C grade individual championship titles were captured by Cheung Shun Wo, Leo
Wan and Cheung Kam Kwong respectively. The Champion House was Ricci （Purple
