## Page Text Content

```text
The Religious Sounds of 71.It was a concert organized by the Serra Club to which
all Catholics were welcomed.
Its aim was to arouse the Christian spirit in the Catholics
of H.K. and to foster vocations. It was divided into sections and a few were held on
the 2oth and 27th of March in our Hall.
by Andrew Ng Yiu Man.
FR. GENERAL ARRUPE AT WAH YAN
```
