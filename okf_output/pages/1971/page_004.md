EDITORIAL
When school opened in September it was found that a new Principal had been
appointed — Fr. Cyril Barrett, S.J. He was not new to Wah Yan as the had been
Rector and Principal from 1956 until 1962, and had already spent last year （1969-70）
on the teaching staff. Fr. Deignan had left Wah Yan and gone to Ricci Hall as War-
den where some of our Senior Students hope to meet him again. We wish him
every success and happiness in his new field of work.
Mrs. Agnes Ng retired from teaching having been on the Wah Yan staff since
1932 with the exception of the war years. Mr. K.M. Chan, and Miss May Mok join-
ed the Staff in September '70. Brother L. Thoo, S.J. left for the U.S.A. in January
and Mr. Gary Lam S.J. joined the Staff. In April Miss Y.Y. Tam left and went
abroad.
December 4th was the day of the Pope's flying visit to Hong Kong and it was
a great thrill for us in Wah Yan, as well as for a great number of Hong Kong folk.
Several hundred of our boys were at the Stadium. We too felt the tension as the
clatter of the helicopter made itself felt coming towards the Valley. Then the man，
Paul, himself! To see him and to hear his voice — his slow, measured tones 一
this was a memory to treasure.
The Students Council continues its work in the school. It has altogether 31
ciubs and societies affiliated to it.
Beginning with the Altar Society the list goes
on to the Bowling Club, Bridge Club, Boxing Club. The list is too long to give in
full, but readers will find in the pages of the Star a report from each society. Each
club and each form from Form 2 to Form Upper Six inclusive sends representatives
to its meetings: There are 54 Councillors in all. The Council appointed Committee
to organize our School Drama Festival and will organize and host this year's Secon-
dary School Students'Seminar.
Our Social Activities Club helped run a week long summer camp for 200 poor
children of Western District; during Xmas vacation a Games' Day was conducted in
Ming Lun Primary School in Taipo with the welccme help of the Social Service Group
of St. Francis Canossian College; and during Chinese New Year holidays the club
held a Fair for pocr children of Wanchai with the helo of the Ying Wa Girls.
Our own School Athletic meet in October '70 revealed more talent than we
had dared to hope for. New records were made in the A grade Shot Put, the C
grade 80m. Low Hurdles, the C grade High Jump and the C grade Shot Put. The
increase in the number of participants in C and D grades gives grounds for hope
of greater athletic success in future. We entered a small team — 15 only — in the
Inter-School Athletic meet at the end of March '71. We gained 2 firsts and enough
places to make us look forward with confidence to the next meet. Our B grade
Badminton won the Championship Cup while our C grade - a completely new team
— got 3rd place.
