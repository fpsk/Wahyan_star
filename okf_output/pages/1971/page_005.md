Our Wah Yan Past Students Dramatic Society is now in the 24th year of its
life. Fr. Sheridan had written the play for the March 1971 performance at the City
Hall and was to have come from Manila to see it through its final rehearsals when
death intervened. Nothing daunted, in the true spirit of their founder, the Dramatic
Society out on their play and played to capacity houses in the City Hall for four nights.

Fr. Pedro Arrupe, Superior General of the Society of Jesus paid a two day
visit to Hong Kong. He met all the Hong Kong and Macau Jesuits on Monday 19th
at our sChool, addressed them and concelebrated Mass with them. He met also a
great number of people who are involved with the Fathers in this work, teachers，
schoolboys and above all the relatives of the members of the Society who were in
Hong Kong.
Of those who met Fr. Arrupe on that occasion not many will forget the
man, small in physical size but immensely big in the warmth and vitality of his per-
sonality.
The Night School still continues. There is less demand for olaces in the lower
classes but the top forms continue to be as full as ever. An old boys' society has
just been started.
At the end of April, agreement was reached with the Pun U Association that
Wah Yan take over their Primary School, which is situated at Shiu Fai Terrace.
supervisor will be our Principal, Fr. Barrett and the Headmaster Fr. Joseph Mallin，
S.J. The school will be known as the Pun U Association Wah Yan Primary School.
The Past Students Association has remained very active. Its aim is to keep
in touch with the past of all the years of the college and to keep the past in touch
with the college. Our Senior students are very grateful for the very useful Careers
Talks organised by the Past.
