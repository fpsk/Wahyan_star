```text
School Drefects
Adviser
•： Mr. S. H. Chau
：Vincent Yu Wing Cheung
Head Prefect
Sub-head Prefect： Peter T'eo
The duty of a School Prefect is to keep the school in good order.
This year we had 9 Prefects from lower six and 6 from F.5 making up a
total of 25. We were glad to have Mr. S.H. Chau as our adviser.
Besides the regular duties at the stair case, along the corridor, in the Tuck Shop
and down the avenue, this year we have started some new routine duties, e.g. in the
School Hall and at the entrance of the corridor leading to the Common Room. We
also hold a major role in keeping order on important occasions such as Graduation
Day, the Annual Ball, Concerts and Debates.
The ‘Sale of Old Books' organised by the School Prefects during the summer
holidays of 1973 was successful. Every Prefect has got a good dinner from the profit.
The rest of the profit is left for organising the same project in 1974.
14 Probationary Prefects from F.4 were chosen to replace those who had left for
the exams.
We must thank most heartily Mr. Chau and the School Authorities in encourag-
ing us and giving us much precious advice. We would also like to express our gratitude
to all those who assisted us.
-53-
```
