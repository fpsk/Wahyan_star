We had 100 members and our club was run successtully.
Activities：—
I）In Oct.， representatives were sent to take part in the social gathering organized by
Tang King Po College and the Diocesan Girls' School.
2） Many volunteers helped on the flag days in Oct.， Dec，， and Mar.
3）In Nov.， a fun fair was organized for Ming Lun Primary School in Taipo. On
28th,Dec.， another fun fair co-organized with the Social Service Group of S.F.C.C.
was held for the poor children in Wanchai. During the Student Festival, a fun fair
for the pupils in the Primary School was held on 10th, March.
4） Six members of ours volunteered to teach in a night tuition centre in Wanchai.
TRE DUCHESS OF KENT CHILDREN'S ORTHOPAEDIC BOSPITAL AND CONVAILESCBNT HOME
5） In Dec.， we had a visit to the Wah Hong Home for the Aged. In Feb.， 27 members
of our club and the Social Service Group visited the Duchess of Kent Children's
Orthpaedic Hospital and Convalescent Home. Besides, we visited the Wah Hong
Home for the Aged with girls from Sacred Heart Canossian College. A joint visit
to ‘The Duchess of Kent Children's Orthopaedic Hospital and Convalescent Home'
with Ying Wah College was also organized.
