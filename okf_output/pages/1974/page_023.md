## Page Text Content

```text
CARITAS
VAH
BAZAAR
’73
-Nov.， 4,1973, started at 10:30 a.m.
- playground, St. Paul's Convent School
- Wah Yan stall （at a corner）
- run by the C.L.C.
- few donations & contributions as usual
- traditional appearance （red bricks, yellow
& blue iron bars）
- complete fresh games
-'attractive"
- greatest eftorts were made by stallers
- crowded, crowded, crowded
- until all exhausted
- a total of $3000 was raised
- thanks to workers
- support us next year
Annual Sall
The Annual Ball was held on the 8th of December in the school hall. Guests
were invited from St. Paul's Convent School and St. Paul's Secondary School. Tickets
were all sold out
a few weeks before the function. This pre-X'mas celebration was
very successful and the gentlemen and ladies present had a very enjoyable time.
— 21—
```
