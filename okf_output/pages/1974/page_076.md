TEE UHSTAEL ATTON OF MORPEER WE LE AS BISEOP
Cathedral of the Immaculate Conception, April, 20, 1974.
BISHOP LEIS SERMON
My dear Brothers and Sisters in Christ，
A year ago we lost our beloved Bishop Francis Hsu.
I have now been called to succeed him to the Diocese of Hong Kong.
To be Bishop of Hong Kong is a great responsibility which becomes
awsome when it involves succeeding two Bishops whom so many of us
remember and loved so much - Bishop Bianchi and Bishop Hsu - two
Bishops of different talents but each of whom gave so much.
It has not been easy for me to accept, and face up to, this responsibilitys it
would be too much for me were it not that I can call upon so much help -
help from God and help from my collaborators, the priests and religious working
with me in this Diocese, and from the faithful whom we serve. All this is a source
of support and inspiration.
To God I pray, especially at this my Installation ceremony, that he will give me the
strength to support my weakness unworthy though Iam. Without Him my work will
be in vain.
To all the priests and religious working with me in Hong Kong of whatever origin
and whatever nationality I give special thanks for their support and loyalty during Che past
year and I am confdent I can count on their continued co-operation during the coming
years In this cosmopolitan society of Hiong Kong, at the beginning of this our plural age，
and in these material times, we need that strong spiritual leadership which can only come
trom a firm unity of all the various talents and experiences which we find today among the
clergy and religious here - a unity which can only grow out of a selfless enthusiasm for
spreading and establishing the word of God in the hearts of the Faithful: and out of a
brotherly love founded on that personal sacrifice to which priests and religious are dedicated
- a brotherly Jove which involves deep charity and care and consideration for others，
irrespective of age, race and outlook - a brotherly love which involves giving more than
receiving
To all our Faichful and to those of our friends not of our Fold I express my apprecia-
tion and gratitude for the support and loyalty you give to our Diocese. Your good will and
Sincerity are my encouragement to take up the appointment to which I have been called.
All these matters give me the assurance and confidence to accept this appointment and
to make my pledge to the Holy See.
Today, in the presence of Archbishop Simon Lourdusamy, Secretary of the Sacred
Congregation for the Lvangel ization of Peoples and of you all, I make my pledge of loyalty
again to the Vicar of Christ, our Holy Father. I sincerely wish that he, as successor of Peter，
who had been entrusted by Our Lord with the task of strengthening his brethren, will
strengthen me, the least of his brethren！
The Bishop, as a father, should be ready to show love and concern to each and every
one; and, as a Pastor, should be prepared to Lead his flock to Christ. Such a rather and
pastor, I wish to be. The Good Shepherd is always ready to give all of himself for his
fock,and I wish to be such a Shepherd. But to be all of this I need your prayers and
God's grace.
Let us all now go forward in unity to promote God's Kingdom, a Kingdom of truth
and life, of holiness and grace, of justice, love and peace.
May almighty God bless us all！
