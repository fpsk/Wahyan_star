ARAINY DAY
One Sunday morning we were to set off to Shek-O for a picnic. Unfortunatey，
just when we were ready, we found dark clouds in the sky. In the next second it began
to rain heavily. How we missed the nice swim we might have ！
We were disappointed and looked gloomily out of the windows. People without
raincoats or umbrellas were running. Some covered their heads with newspapers and
others ran until they got shelter. The unfortunate ones were badly splashed by cars.
Most buses, trams and taxi-cabs were full. There were shelters too. What we saw made
us laugh and forget our disappointment.
