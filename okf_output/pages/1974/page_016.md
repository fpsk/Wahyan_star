Mr. FRANCIS NG LUT WAI
Francis Ng had almost reached 60 when he died in August 1973. As a boy he
was at school in St. Joseph's College and later in La Salle. He did teacher training and
was invited by Brother Aimar to teach in La Salle College, Boundary Street. During
the Japanese occupation after a short interlude working on ships, Francis found it
expedient to leave Hong Kong and go back to the family village near Nam Hoi. There，
he and his wife conducted a school for the village children. Soon after the end of the
Pacific War, he made his way back to Hong Kong and joined the staff of Wah Yan at
Robinson Rd. As a teacher he was quiet, steady and hardworking. In 1951 he became
ill with cancer of the throat. Fortunately, the disease responded well to deep X-ray
treatment and he was able to teach without diliculty. There was no recurrence until
June 1973 when he began to feel unwell. The doctors did everything possible, but had
to admit that he had only a month to live. He took the verdict calmly and set about
preparing for death. He said more than once that he was thankful to God that he had
not died in 1951 when his children were small but that he was able to live to see them
grown up and able to make their way in the world. He was grateful too that his
children were so good and loving towards their parents. He was greatly consoled dur-
ing those last days that his wife and children were all the time in attendance at his
bedside.
Francis has left his family and all of us the example of a good life which is more
precious than gold.
