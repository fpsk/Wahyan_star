It is refreshing to be able to report on our Association's achievements during the
year ended 30th April 1974, due to the efforts of the Council headed by Mr. Jeffrey
Sun, particularly the Hon. Sec. Mr. HO Wai-kam and Hon. Treasurer Mr. David H.C
LO, as well as the wise guidance of President Peter K.P. Hall and Rev. Father Barrett.
1. Opening Ceremony of Swimming-shed at South Bay: This was held on a
bright Sunday in May 1973, with the usual flag-raising by President Peter Hal, assisted
by councillor Mr. TSE Ping-shan and other zealous colleagues. About 100 participants，
old and young, enjoyed tremendously the pleasant trips on tourist-bus and private cars，
the warm sea-water, and also the lovely cakes/sandwiches/soft drinks freely provided by
President Hall.
