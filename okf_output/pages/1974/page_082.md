2T9092
FOOTBALL REVIEW
LEAGUE CHAMPIONS - Our sChool A Grade Team had never got the title
since 1952/53 & 1961/62. However, this year, our ‘A' Team remained undefeated in
the League after beating St. Stephen's Church （2:1）， St. Paul's Boys （6:1）， Holy Child
（8:0） & Salesian School （3:1）， Dut having a tie with St. Louis School： （0:0）， and eventually
won the League Champion 1973/74.
In the 7-aside knock-out competition, we were unfortunately knocked out by La
Salle College by 4:3 in a match ended with additional time, after beating St. Paul's
Boys again by 1:0.
In the 1l-aside knock-out competition, we defeated Raimondi College by 2:0 &
Baldwin College by 4:0. But we were in hard luck again in the quarter final when
we were eliminated by Kowloon New Method by penalty kicks after a match which end-
ed with a draw in 3:3.
The B Grade & C Grade Teams, having tried their best, did not do very well
in the League and failed to enter in the Knock-out Competitions Final. Most of them
lacked experience since they were "new blood”.
Finally, we would like to thank Fr. Corbally for his encouragement, Mr.Ip Fuk
Wah & Mr. Li Sai Kuen for being our team-coaches.
A Grade
‘Match'
C Grade
B Grade
—80 —
