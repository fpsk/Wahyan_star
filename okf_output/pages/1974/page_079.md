CHRISTIAN LIFE COMMUNITY（Junior）
Ecclesiastical Assistant: Rev. Fr. Brosnan S.J.
Prefect: Paulus Lau （4A）
Meetings were held weekly in which an instruction course was conducted by the
ecclesiastical assistant. We attended a special mass in the school chapel once a month.
Activities：—
1） Stamp Selling - held between 26th and 29th of March. $380 were collected for
educational progress in Zambia.
2） Discussion Day - a whole day discussion was held in the Stanley Retreat House
during the Chinese New Year holidays. The theme was "Vocation' and a talk was
given by Rev. Fr. Luke Tsui.
3） The Tth General Assembly of the Federation of C.L.C. was held during Christmas
holidays in the Diocean Youth Centre, one representative from us attended this
3 days'conference.
4） Annual Day Rally - held on 24th March in Wan Yan College Kowloon. Some of
us attended it.
5） We also took part in many functions organized by the Senior C.L..C. - bazaar，
Christmas carolling, chapel choir, lunch time concerts, holy hours, visit to hospital
and publication of monthly leaflets.
—77—
