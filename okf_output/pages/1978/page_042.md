## Page Text Content

```text
MUSIC
SOCIETY
Adviser：
Fr. McCarthy, S.J.
Chairman: Lai Cheung Wah, Daniel
This year the Music Society got through a
very heavy programme of events.In
October we presented our first concert
“Evening Serenade”
.Many guests were
invited to perform in this concert.
“Talent Time '77" was held in Decem-
ber. There were very keen competitions and
the standard of competitors was generally
high.
“Talent Time Winners Night” was the
highlight of the year. The winners of the
five. sections competed again in order to find
the champion. This award was won by
Chan Chung Ming and his Group by their
song “The Marvellous Toy".
In March, our society, with the Chinese
Society, jointly organized a Chinese Cultural
Ihere was a great success. A very
large audience attended and the entertain-
ment was much appreciated.
The Music Society arranged
Lunch-Time Concerts. The purpose of these
concerts was to develop students' interests in
all types of music. Representatives of our
school participated in the “Talent Quest”
jointly organized by M.S.S.， S.P.S.S.， and
Raimondi College. We also sent performers
to the Secondary Schools' Invitation Concert
organzed by Hornell Hall and the H.K.
University.
We would like to take this opportunity
to say a very sincere word of thanks to all
those who in any way contributed to the
work of the Society during the year.
```
