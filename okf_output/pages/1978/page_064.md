STUDENT
FESTIVAL '78
very year, the Student Counci will
E organize an important function in order
to bring the activities of our school to a
climax. This year, the Student Festival 78
was launched. It began on March 4th and
ended on March, 18th.
The Student Festival ‘78 was a joint
production of the Student Council, Social
Service Society, Chinese Society and
Photography Club. During the fortnight, a
fun fair, an Interschool Mixed teachers-
students Debate, an Interschool Quadran-
gular Basketball Invitation Competition, an
Interschool Games Competition, an Inter-
house Games Competition, an Interclass
Treasure Hunt, an Interclubs Death Penalty
Competition, the Teachers Day,Lunch
Time Concerts （organized by the Student
Council）. Interschool Social Work Exhibi-
tion, a Walk for a Million, a film show
about social work （organized by the
Social Service Society）， a Chinese Culture
Concert, a Book Exhibition, a Chinese
Calligraphy Competition （organized by the
Chinese Society） and a Camera Exhibition
（organized by the Photography Club）
were organized.
Finally, we would like to express our
sincere thanks to all those who have helped
to make the Student Festival 78 a success.
Walk For Mon, Walk For Fun
