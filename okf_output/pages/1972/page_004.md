EDITORIAL
What made September '71 different from all other Wah Yan Septembers was the
coming into being of Pun U Association Wah Yan Primary School. It is situated at
Shiu Fai Terrace which is not far away from the mother school. More about Pun U
Wah Yan in the pages that follow.
There were a number of staff changes：
Mr. Choy Heung Ming Joined the staff in September 1971, taking the place of Mr. Chan
Kim Man who went to Canada for further studies.
Mrs. Cissy Lau, because of family affairs, gave up fultime teaching but continued as
form-mistress on a part-time basis. She was replaced Miss Agness Cheung Yuen
Har in September 1971.
Mr. Bosco Cho, a past student, Joinea the staff in February 1972 on a temporary basis.
Mr. Vincent Lee was selected to study the special third year mathematics course at
Northcote College of Education. During his absence his place was taken by Mr.
Johnson Liao, also a past student.
Gary Lam, who had joined the staff early in 1971 left us at the end of December and
went to Australia.
The night school carries on with some difficulty and with a reduced number
of Classes. Girls are now admitted ana there are now a good year but there are reasons
to hope for a brighter future.
On December 2Ist the Wah Yan Jesuit Community Sufferes the loss by death of
one of their members Father Sean Turner. A great many past students mourn the
passing of one whom they rightly regarded as a great scholar.
