# Wah Yan Star 1972 - Page 12


## Page Text Content

```text
Onoocations
The name "Wah Yan”， meaning "Fair （elegant, exquisite，—Chinese） Humanity”
sugsests another appeliation for the Blessed Virgin.
Empress of Angels, whose star-spurning throne
Nearest the All-Holy rises—Arbitress
Of grace and gentle godliness；—alone
Of beings innumerable to possess
Blood-fellowship with God! For all thy excess
Of glory, yet thy obedient courtesy
Marks thee, O Mother of Eternal Light, no less
Most gracious Mother of Humanity.
Fairness incomparable-no cloudy zone
Veiling the sun with radiant fleeciness
But pales by thee. Not almond-blossoms blown
On snows by cold spring winds, in loveliness
One millioneth glow so pure. Yet co'st thou—yes，
Even from the utimate fringe of Deity，
To comfort sin-stained mortals' feebleness，—
Most gracious Mother of Humanity！
Unwearied advocate of them that groan
For guilt; kind temperer of all distress，—
Still sweetest fruits, in thy Child's suffering sown，
Fall from thy hands, which，—bounteous in largess，
Are yet invincible to clean depress
Foul field, proud puritan, and pharisee.
—Poor sinner's hope and map of sinlessness，
Most gracious Mother of Humanity！
Envoi
Prince Everlasting, Thou didst royally bless
Thy humble handmaid.—Humble thanks to Thee
That mad'st Thy Maiden Nurse and Comfortress
Most gracious Mother of Humanity.
J. A. Turner S.J.
```
