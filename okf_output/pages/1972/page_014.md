## Page Text Content

```text
OBITUARY
Rev. Fr. John Turner, S. J.
Father Turner died at Wah Yan College on Dec. 2lst 1971, aged 62. He had been on
our teaching staff from 1951 until 1954 and again from 1962 until 1964 when ill health
compelled him to give up teaching. He was a learned man in the lore of East and West
and as will be seen on other pages in this issue was not only a poet but also an artist.
```
