SPOR TS DAY：
The 3ist annual athletic meet was
held on December 1971 at the Hong Kong
Stadium. Three records were broken, all
in ‘B grade. Both Lam Sui Lung and
Leung Kam Kwong who came first and
second in the 1500m broke the old record.
The 4x100m relay was broken by Green
House with a time of 52 sec. A complete
list of winners is given elsewhere.
St. Joseph's College won our Invita-
tion relay, followed by Wah Yan, Kow-
loon and Raimondi.
Wah Yan Sports and Gala days al-
ways run oft very smoothly and eficiently.
We expect them to do so, but this requires
a lot of meticulous organisation. Thank
Here I Come！
You, Mr. Chung！
The Winning Invitation Relay Teams
