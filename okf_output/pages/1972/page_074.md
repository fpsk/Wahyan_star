In the second inter-school debate, we
had Wah Yan College against Saint Paul's
Secondary School. It was held on the 10th
ofDecember at half-past four in Saint
Paul's Secondary School. The motion was
“That a Much Stricter Penal Code Would
Reduce the Amount of Crime in Hong
Kong”". Saint Paul's Secondary School was
for the motion and we were against it.
The adjudicators were Father McGaley
（from our school）and Mrs. Patel （from
Saint Paul's）.
The government got 647
while the opposition got 663. So the mo-
tion was defeated.
Speak up！！
Anthony Wu Li Chi
Him and Vincent Ho were on the School
Team.
The third inter-school debate was
again a mixed one with Sacred Heart Ca-
nossian College. It was held on the 6th of
January at half-past Four in our School
Hall. The motion was "that the money
spent on the Hong Kong Festival is worth-
spending"with Catherine Poon, Hu Fai
Chung, Katherine Hui annd Alfred Chu
representing the government and Alan Lo，
Ellen Szeto, Li Chi Fai and Grace Chow
on the opposition. The motion was de-
feated by a narrow margin. The adjudica-
tors were Mrs. Chung from S.H.C.C.and
Father O'Rourke.
Try a little kindness.
The fourth interschool debate was
held on the 3rd of March at half-past four
in our School Hall.
We had Wah Yan
College on the government side and Beli-
lios Public School was on the opposition.
The speakers from our school were Louis
Huang, Philip Ho and Henry Cheung. The
motion was “That Money is NOT an Es-
sential Factor in a Happy Marriage."The
adjudicators were Father McCarthy and
Mr. Poon from BPS. The motion was de-
feated.
Much of the year's success was due to
the dedication and ceaseless initiative of
our adviser, Father McGaley, to conclude，
we must thank Father McCarthy, Father
consented to be the judges at the inter-
class and inter-school debates and also our
Assistant Principal, Mr. Yu who saw to it
that the school hall was ready for use be-
fore each debate.
The Judge
