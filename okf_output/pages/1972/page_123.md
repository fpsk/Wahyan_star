BASKETBALL ‘A’GRADE
This year was rather an unlucky year
for the team.
Except for a few, most
players are Form V boys who are going to
sit for the School Certificate Exam. As a
result, the players had to spend their time
preparing for the Exam. rather than at-
tending basketball practices. Actually, our
team practised twice only throughout the
whole year. Added to this, our team, dur-
ing the league matches, was put into the
strongest division in which were Lingnam
College, the present champions and St.
Paul's Boys' College in the same division
and we met our Waterloo once again.
However, the enthusiasm of the team was
undoubtedly great and I would like to ex-
press my heartfelt thanks to our sportmas-
ter -Rev. Fr. Corbally, our coach，
Thomas Ho Pui Kun and all our players
for their tremendous enthusiasm and co-
operation.
Eddie Chan Sui Ki，
Captain.
