SWIMMING GALA：
What are you waiting for？
This was held on Sept. 29 at Victoria
Pool which had been specially reserved.
The Gala started at 9a.m. and ended
around ncon. It was a rainy day and the
water was cold. The Lite Guard Club
sent a patrol to be responsible for the
safety of the competitors. The number of
entries was about the same as last year，
but due to the bad weather a lot of swim-
mers dropped out trom the events. Teach-
ers and Fathers acted as adjudicators.
During the intermission the Life
Guard Club members gave a demonstration
on how to save a drowning person.
The day ended with a competition be-
tween teachers and students. One record
was broken this year. The results are
given below.
Who'll stop the rain？
Douglas Van
