OBIIUAKY
Robert Lim Gek Bor was a pupil of
Wah Yan College, Hong Kong until July，
1936 when he matriculated. At first he
did some temporary teaching until the
War broke out in December, 1941.
After the War he entered Government
Service and remained until he retired at
the age of 55. He was, however, invited
back to Government and remained until
about two years ago when he finally
retired.
Robert became a teacher in our Wah
Yan Night School in 1946. He continued
for more than 20 years, most of the time
as a Form I teacher of English. That he
was hignly successful was quite plain from
the way boys promoted from Primary 6
wanted to enter Mr. Lim's class and no
other.
Father Joseph Howatson was born in Co. Kerry, Ireland in 1910. He
entered the Society of Jesus in 1927. In 1937 he came to Wah Yan to teach
and to act as games-master. In 1945 after studies and ordination in Ireland
he returned to Wah Yan where he remained as teacher and procurator until
1952. It was during this time that he founded the Wah Yan Shoeshine Club
which flourished for more than thirty years. It was this work which gave him
his interest in Social Work which was to he one of the chief interests of his
life. The work he loved most and did most for was the Boys' and Girls' Clubs
Association.
From 1962 on he was an invalid. He died in hospital on August 23ra，
1972.
