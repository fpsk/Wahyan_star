REPORT OF BADMINTON
This year the Badminton players
of Wah Yan witnessed an era in the
badminton pool of their own school.
The Sportsmaster, Fr. Corbaly decided
to give us coaching.
Mr. Wong Fai
Hung, a most renowned and skilful
player in the Colony, very kindly agreed
to be the Coach.
The A' Team had a good run this
year, losing once to La Salle in the
League and beating all the others. We
thought we had secured the Runners-
up, but it so happened that La Salle
lost to St. Paul's Boys （whom we had
beaten）， and when the actual scoring of
the games was taken into considera-
tion, we ended up only as Third.
In the Knock-out Competition, as
our former captain was abroad, two of
the players were occupied with Certi-
ficate studies, and another player broke
his arm, we let the ‘B' boys take over.
They secured the Runners-up in the B
League.
The ‘C' Grade shuttlers in spite of
their keenness in the coaching, did not
finish off the season with flying colours.
And definitely, as in many other games
in Wah Yan, we need some new blood.
Looking forward to yet another in-
teresting season, we would like to thank
Fr. Corbally for his attention and en-
couragement, Mr. Wong Fai Hung for
his coaching, and Mr. Tsang, Mr.
Cheung, and Mr. Lee for being team
masters.
