SPEECH DAY
Wah Yan staff and pupils were fortunate to
be able to welcome an alumnus of thirty-one
years ago, Mr. Li Fook Kau, to give the address
on Speech Day.
Y
Mr. Li, recently appointed Director of the
Social Welfare Department, reminded the
graduates that they were citizens of a great city
which had come a long way from its entrepot
days when he was a schoolboy. It was some-
thing for them to be proud of that Hong Kong，
without natural resources, had accomplished so
much in terms of industrial strength and im-
proved standards of living. This success was，
no doubt, due to the mixture of East and West
in Hong Kong's composition. This mixture is
seen in the city's social welfare feld also: we
have the traditional institutions like the Tung
Wah Group and the Po Leung Kuk along with
the newer welfare agencies.
Both groups by
their co-operation will continue to serve the
needs of Hong Kong people. Mr. Li pointed
out that though Social Welfare work needs
money, the equally important need is personal
involvment and compassion for the needy and
the suffering. The graduates going out from
school to help in Hong Kong's development
should themselves make a contribution to the
welfare work in our community; this will mean
donating money and time at all stages of their
careers. Some graduates will no doubt take up
social work as a career. Even if they do not
occupy themselves in other fields, they will
do well to find an outlet for their concern for
the deprived in the many voluntary organiza-
tions in Hong Kong.
