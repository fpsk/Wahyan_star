```text
The Debating Society
Adviser
：Rev. Fr. McGaley, S.J.
Chairman
：Anthony Wu Ting Yuk
（L6A）
Vice-Chairman: Lee Chi Him （F.5A）
Activities：-
From September to November we held
an interclass contest between F.4 to F.6.
4A, 5A and L6S were the champions. In
April, there was again a F.3 interclass de-
bate competition. 3A was the winner.
This year we had 6 interschool de-
bates with SHGG, SPSS, SPCS, BPS, and
WYK.
In March, an English-Chinese mixed
debate between the staffs and students of
St. Paul's Secondary School, St. Francis
Cannossian College and our school was
organized to celebrate the Student Festi-
val. More than 750 from the 3 schools and
other gquest school schools packed the hall.
This kind of debate was an innovation in
Hong Kong.
- 68 —
```
