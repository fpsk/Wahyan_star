## Page Text Content

```text
Football Report
Before the league matches started our
A-Grade team was encouraged by defeat-
ing Wah Yan Kowloon （2 to 1） in a friend-
Iy match. In the league St. Louis defeated
us by 1-0, and then Caine College, the
league champion, beat us by 5-0. Not the
least bit discouraged we went on to play
Literary College and to beat them by 2-0.
Our league competition ended by two
drawn matches with Salesian School and
St. Paul's Boys' College. Since the players
were mostly in Form V and exam pressure
made itself felt, we gave up the knock-out
competition.
It was a good season. Our players though
busy at studies were co-operative and all
master and to our coach, Mr. S. M. Tsang
who gave us of his best.
Our B-Graders got a high position in
the league but were defeated in the knock-
out.
```
