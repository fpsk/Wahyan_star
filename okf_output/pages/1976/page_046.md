## Page Text Content

```text
School
Prefeets
Adviser
：Mr. S. H. Chan
Head Prefect：Dennis Au Kin Kwok （L6A）
Sub-head Prefect: Stephen Lam Sai Ming （L6A）
This year there were altogether twenty-seven prefects: fifteen from Lower Form Six
and the others from Form Five. But two Lower Six prefects left school in the early part of
the term.
We are glad to have Mr. Chau Sui Hay as our adviser. Under his guidance，
we were able to run everything smoothly.
Fourteen Probationary Prefects from Form Four were able to replace the Form Five
Prefects who lett for their exams.
Besides the regular daily duties, we also played an important role in keeping good
order and discipline at various functions such as Speech Day, Swimming Gala, Annual Ball，
Concerts, Lectures and Debates. One pretect was responsible for every two Chasses trom Form
One to Form Three by helping them to solve their problems.
In DecemDer, our pretects were invited by the pretects of B.P.S. to a folk dance.
Everyone enjoyed the dance very much. In February we had a football match with our
"Brother School'- Wah Yan Kowloon. We had a delicious supper after the match.
The “Sale of old Books' organized by the Prefects during the summer holiday was very
successful. Our prefects were also in charge of a Christmas Tea Party for boys of Form
one to Form three so as to bring better understanding between Prefects and junior boys.
We are much indebted to Mr. Chau and the School Authorities for encouraging us
and giving us much invaluable advice.
```
