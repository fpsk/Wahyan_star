6. Career talks were given to the College's senior students by three of our profes-
sionals among the Past Students, and these were：
Mr. Raymond WONG on “Accountancy”，
Mr. Raphael CHAN on “Law"， and
Mr. Jeffrey SUN on “Business Administration”.
The talks were warmly accepted as subjects of tremendous interest and practical
value.
7. Our major social event of the year - Annual Ball at HK. Hilton Grand Ballroom
on 23rd Dec. 1975 - scored another triumph in all respects; the excellent enter-
tainment programme and the lucky-draw were fully appreciated by the “capacity"
participants, comprising past students and their former teachers with their family
members as well as some distinguished guests from the Wah Yans - another ex-
ample of our “solidarity” amidst gay atmosphere and festive spirit, with Goodwill
towards Men！
8. The traditional Graduation Dinner, held immediately after the College Speech-Day
events, was a memorable joint-effort function. The Association arranged for the
dinner and incidental matters, while the Graduates provided the entertainment pro-
gramme and were rewarded with prizes from a Lucky-draw.
9. A gay Spring-party, held at Franho Restaurant soon after the Chinese New Year，
was looked upon by all participants as a fitting occasion to exchange greetings and
good wishes, apart from happy reminiscence of the Good Old Days at school and
in the outside world.
10. A newly-published Members' Directory of 90 pages, with full lists of our current
office-bearers, Hon. Presidents, Hon. Advisers, Hon. Auditor and Hon. Legal Ad-
viser, plus “A word from the Editor"， containing a brief history of the Association
（from the fond memories of our veterans and a founder-member of the Associa-
tion），will give the reader some idea of the diversified “occupational” positions of
our New and Old Members, among whom is a Graduate from the Alma Mater
in 1923！
