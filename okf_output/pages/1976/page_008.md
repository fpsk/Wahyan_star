Puncpals Message
Our Rector, Fr. Cronin returned in August 1975, having been in hospital
in London for some months. The Opening Mass of the year was celebrated
by Fr. Marciano Baptista S.J.， a local Jesuit just ordained. There was a
large gathering for the occasion in the School Hall.
About 150 new boys entered the school, including 100 from the Primary
section, Pun U Wah Yan. We began the new year with two new teachers.
Fr. Sean O Cearbhallain, S.J.， who had taught here before, and Mr. K.C. Kwok，
a science teacher who took the place of Mr. K.C. Fung. Mr. Fung had taught
lor more than 15 years in the upper school from Form 4 to Upper Form 6.
He had finally reached the age when no further extension was possible.
During the first term we entered about 100 boys for English and Chinese
verse and prose reading. They obtained many certificates and quite a few
awards. Our junior （C Grade） tabletennis team won the inter-school competi-
tion and both our senior basketball team and handball team were victorious
in their competitions.
Illness dogged the school stalf in the latter part of the year. Both Fr.
Lawler and Fr. Corbally spent several weeks in hospital. Mr. K.P. Lee has off
teaching for four months and Mrs. Agnes Tam for several weeks. Finally, as
we go to press, both the school secretary, Mr. Joseph Lo, and the office atten-
dant， "Fai Pak”， are recovering in hospital. Thanks are due to all who stood
in for the absentees.
We record with thanks the following donations received during the year
from past students, Yee-Sun Wu and Tieh-Yee Wu, Charitable Foundation -
$25,000 for scholarships. Mr. Patrick P.K. Wu $10,000 for scholarships.
Mr. Cheng Siu Wan -$12,000 in memory of his mother, for school
equipment. Mr. Ng Chun Ho - a pair of amplifiers; Mr. Maurice Lee - an
air conditioner for the Sports Office; Mr. Francis Lee - $5,000 for a school
scholarship.
