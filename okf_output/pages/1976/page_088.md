BASKETBALL
A GRADEー
THE INTERSCHOOL CHAMPIONS
It was really a lucky year for us. Although we could not play with our fulk
line-up in even one single match, we managed to get the championship.
In the first round, our team remained undefeated urtil we met New Method
College.
As we were already qualified to the finals, we did not want to challenge them
with all our efforts. So we lost that match.
In the final round, we first encountered Pui Kiu Middle School, a team full of
strength and force. But we were fit enough to beat them by 6 points. Next we had
to face International School. Their whole team was composed of foreigners, who were
alsolutery supperior to us in physique and strength, though not in skill. We lost. How-
ever, we gathered our spirit and won 3 successive matches against New Method College，
Rosaryhill School and Queen's College.
The result was that the 3 top
teams: Wah Yan, Pui Kiu and International were level in their marks. Replay matches
were needed to dletermine the champion team. But at that time, it was found that In-
ternational School had broken the regulations of the Interschool Competition.
They
were therefore disqualified. That means, we lost no match in the fnals, and automati-
cally, we got the championship - a victory that had eluded W'ah Yan for 15 years, since
1961. Come on, my dear fellows, try your best to retain the crown next year！
