The Association has made some signifcant achievements during the year ended
3Ist May 1976, and a summary of them is given below for the information of our well-
wishers：—
1. Some fnancial contribution was made towards the publication of the “STARLET"
marking a close link between the Old and the Young Wall-Yanites.
2. Our Swimming Shed at South Bay was completely renovated, with further improve-
ments and additions, including a high railing with wire-mesh on the roof and a
metal ladder leading up there, plus a magnificent flag pole for ceremonial occa-
sions.
All these have made a welcome change in the Shed, which is so popular
among members and students alike. Our annual “Opening Ceremony”was held
with fag-raising, and raffles and a tea-party added to the fun of the occasion.
3. The Reunion Dinner Party at Franho Restaurant, Wanchai, held prior to the
Annual General Meeting, proved most successful in renewing friendships over wine
glasses amidst gay atmosphere, the "new blood”meeting the “old-timers" for gay
Chats, and in majong and other games.
4. A Launch Picnic to Chikpik near Clear Water Bay was well attended and
thoroughly enjoyed by some 90 participants, among whom were many small kids.
Lively music and songs were provided throughout, plus an exciting Lucky-draw
of attractive prizes - a refreshing event in the intense summer heat！
5. Our 3rd Annual Inter-Wal Yan Pingpong Tournament took place in October
1975 at Wah Yan,Kowloon, Champions of the preceding year. Contestants in-
cluded the usual 6 teams from the Students of Pun U Wah Yan Primary School，
Wal Yan HONGKONG and KOWLOON, Ricci Hall and the two Past Students'

Associations （HK./KOWLOON）. After very keen contests，
