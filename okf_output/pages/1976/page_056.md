```text
Photography Club
Adviser
： Rev. Fr. P. Toner, S. J.
Chairman
： Thomas Lam Man Pan （L6A）
Vice-chairman: John Lee Cho Hon （5B）
Secretary
：Muller Tang Hee Wah （L6S）
Treasurer
： Chan Yuen Wing （5B）
Dark-room
Manager
：Andy Chu Kam Tong （L6A）
Activities：
This year we had a greater variety of activities though being less in quantity than
that of the previous year.
1. In the beginning of the academic year, the members were taught how to print and
develop photos.
2.
Lectures - we had two lectures this year. The first one was given by Mr. Tam
Wing Yat on 10 November, 1975 in the Music Room.
The topic was on the basic
techniques on photography. Exactly four months later, at the same place, another
lecture was given by Mr. Tam Ning on dark-room technique.
3. Contests - There were two contests held in December and May respectively. Our
adviser kindly acted as our judge.
4.
Outing - The first outing took place at the Peak in the beginning of November.
The second one was a joint-school function. Our guests were from Maryknoll Sisters'
School who exchanged ideas with us and kindly acted as models. Our destination
was Shing Mun Reservoir and the date was 27 March.
5.
Visits - Our first visit since 1971 was to the photo-fnishing lab of Kodak （Far East）
Co.， it was on 20 April. Not more Lnan two days later, we paid another visit to
the camera manufacture factory of Yashica
Co.in Kwun Tong. We were accompanied
by the Maryknollers in these visits.
Here，
we would like to express our sincere thanks
to the two companies for arranging these
visits.
6.
There will be a photo-training course in
the summer vacation for
the students.
Outings, lectures and discusions are to be
included in this course.
Here, we would like to send our warmest
thanks to Fr. Toner who kindly acted as our
adviser during the year.
```
