Its aim was to provide training in bridge playing.

（2） Student Fete Tournament
It provided a parctice for the members and the non-members.
It was held on 20th March 1976 in our Geography Room. There were
about 30 students who participated in the tournament and medals were
given to the champion pair.
（3） Easter Open Pairs Tournament
Its aim was to encourage the members to participate in open tourna-
ments.
It was held on I9th April 1976 in the school hall. Many students from
other schools were invited to take part and there were totally 80 persons
Who participated. Refreshment was provided after the tournament.
