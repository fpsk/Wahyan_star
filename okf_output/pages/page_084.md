SPORTS REVIEW OF THE YEAR （19Z5-26）
I have a feeling that Wah Yan boys are indifterent in everything. In the sphere
of sports, there is no exception.
SPECTATORS ARE OUR LIFE
Compared with other boys' schools, few students in Wah Yan are interested in
sports. And even for this limited number of players, their morale is not high. The
lowering of the spirit is probably due to the lack of ‘a sense of glory' to be good at
sports. Being school representatives, we get nothing special, and nothing good. Al-
though we say we take part in sports for sports only, not for any other reasons, awards
are sometimes essential, By the word “awards,I do not mean material ones, but
spiritual ones. Take for an example, even in the final matches of A grade Interschool
basketball and headball, no more than 50 spectators from Wah Yan came to support
us. Really, how discouraging it was. We got the championship, but only a few in the
school know, and nobody cares. I am not asking for praise and glory. What I am
dlemanding is pretty simple: just to come and cheer us on. But why are so many of
youl so Slow to do this？
TRANSITIONAL STAGE
In these years, Wah Yan is in a transitional stage. The senior players are do-
ing well, capturing interschool championship in basketball, football and handball. But
these players are growing old and leaving Wah Yan. However, the B grade players
are not brilliant enough to succeed them. So, the intermediate schoolteams usually get
unsatistactory results. But you do not have to worry, because now, a new generation
has grown up. Can't you see that our “junior' boys are doing so well this year, namely
in table tennis, badminton as well as swimming：
